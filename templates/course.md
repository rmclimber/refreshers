## How to use this refresher

**For:** What the reader once knew, and what remains familiar.

**Afterward:** Two to four concrete things they should be able to do.

**Scope:** Prerequisites, included topics, and deliberate omissions.

**Route:** A short path for a fast pass and a route for deeper review.

### Diagnostic

Use a few representative tasks to expose different kinds of rust. Point to relevant modules; give answers in the answer key.

## Mental model

Restore the central relationships before cataloguing details.

## Core topic

### A high-leverage construction or concept

**Pattern / model:** State the structure.

**Use:** Explain when and why it matters.

- A realistic example with explanation or translation.
- A second example showing a useful variation.

**Pitfall / contrast:** Identify a predictable mistake and how to repair it. State the limit of any shortcut.

## Apply it

One or two compact integrated tasks. Give a concrete output and success criteria.

## Answer key

Answers to the diagnostic and worked models or evaluation criteria for the tasks.

## Quick reference

A short lookup section that points back to explanations.

## Sources and further review

Reliable, linked sources with the topic each supports. Identify review status and any unresolved issues.
